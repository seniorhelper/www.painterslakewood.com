Vaulted ceilings are the Lakewood variable

A great deal of Lakewood's housing stock, particularly the 1980s and 90s builds through Green Mountain, Solterra and the Rooney Valley side, has vaulted or two-story entry ceilings. They change an interior project more than square footage does.

Height means staging. A room with a flat eight-foot ceiling is ladder work. A two-story entry with a stairwell beneath it needs proper scaffolding set over the stair treads, and setting that safely is time spent before any coating is applied. If a bid for your house looks identical to a bid for a single-level ranch, the staging is missing from it.

It also means the cut line at the ceiling is twenty feet above your eye. Done carelessly it is visible from the front door for the next decade.

What the day actually looks like

Painting is the quick part. Two coats on a bedroom is an afternoon. The work that decides whether it looks professional happens first:

  • Floors covered, furniture moved and protected, fixtures and hardware masked or removed.
  • Nail holes, dings and corner cracks filled, then sanded flush.
  • Caulk where trim meets wall, which is the line your eye reads as tidy or sloppy.
  • Spot primer over every patch so it does not flash through the finish.
  • Hand-cut lines at ceilings, corners and trim.

Garden levels and walkout basements

Lakewood is full of split-level and walkout designs where the lower floor is partly below grade. Those rooms need checking before paint: efflorescence on a foundation wall, the white powdery deposit you sometimes see, means moisture is moving through the masonry. Coating over it traps the problem. We would rather find it at the estimate than after.

Winter is the right time

Our exterior calendar loses half the year to weather. Interiors do not. From November through March the schedule is open, dates are flexible, and you are not competing with everyone in Jefferson County for the same July week.

Questions

Interior painting questions

How much does interior painting cost in Lakewood?

Published 2026 Front Range data puts interior painting at roughly $1.50 to $3.50 per square foot, and most homes are quoted by room. Ceiling height is the biggest single variable in Lakewood, because vaulted and two-story spaces need staging rather than a ladder.

Do you paint vaulted ceilings and two-story entries?

Yes, and it is routine here. Those spaces need proper scaffolding, often set over stair treads, which is reflected in the estimate rather than discovered partway through.

Can you paint during winter?

Yes, and it is the smarter time. Interior work happens inside a heated house, so the outdoor temperature is irrelevant and scheduling is far easier between November and March.

Do I need to move furniture myself?

We move and protect larger pieces. Clearing small items, electronics and artwork beforehand speeds the work and can reduce your cost.

What about my basement walls?

Finished basements paint like any room. Bare foundation or block needs masonry primer, and any efflorescence means moisture is moving through and should be addressed before coating.

How long does a full interior take?

A single room is usually one day. A whole house typically runs three days to two weeks depending on size, ceiling height, trim detail and repairs. The estimate gives you the real schedule.
