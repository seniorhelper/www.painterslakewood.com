02

Three things specific to this city

Stucco is half the west side

Solterra, Rooney Valley and much of Green Mountain are stucco, and stucco is not siding with a texture. It is porous and has to breathe. Coat it with the wrong product and trapped moisture pushes the film off, sometimes taking stucco with it. We choose coatings by vapor permeability and repair cracks by type before anything else. How we handle stucco.

The hogback drives the maintenance cycle

West Lakewood sits where the plains meet the foothills. Afternoon sun hits those elevations unobstructed, and downslope wind funnels through the gaps carrying grit. West and south walls here commonly need attention a full cycle before north and east. Phasing a repaint that way is a legitimate way to spread cost, and we will quote it.

Hillside lots and vaulted rooms mean staging

Green Mountain lots step down the slope, so a wall that is one story at the front is two or more at the back. Inside, vaulted great rooms and two-story entries need scaffolding rather than ladders. Both take real time to set safely, and both belong in the estimate rather than surfacing midway through.

Lakewood painting questions

Who are the best painters in Lakewood, CO?

Judge the paperwork, not the pitch. Before you choose, make each bidder hand over proof of insurance, a scope that spells out every prep step, the named product and sheen, a coat count and readable warranty terms. Bighorn Painting puts all five in writing and holds 4.9 stars from 95 Google reviews.

How much does it cost to paint a house in Lakewood?

Published 2026 Front Range data puts exterior house painting at roughly $1.55 to $4.10 per square foot of paintable surface and interior painting at about $1.50 to $3.50 per square foot. Stucco repair and hillside access are the factors most likely to move a Lakewood number.

Do you paint stucco?

Yes, and it is a significant part of what we do here. Stucco needs coatings selected for vapor permeability and crack repair matched to the crack type. Painting it like siding is why many stucco jobs fail.

Are you insured?

Yes, and the certificate arrives with your quote rather than on request. With no state painting licence in Colorado, that document plus a detailed written scope is the whole of your protection.

What paint brands do you use?

We work in Sherwin-Williams and PPG. Each surface on your estimate carries the product line and sheen by name, so the manufacturer's own data sheet is a search away.

When can you paint my exterior?

Realistically from about the third week of May until the first week of October. Local climate normals put the last spring freeze around May 5 and the first autumn freeze around October 7. Watch the overnight minimum rather than the afternoon high: the film is still curing while you sleep.

What does the 5-year warranty cover?

The labour side: how the surface was readied and how the material went on. Should that fail inside five years, we come back and fix it. Separately, the coating itself carries whatever the manufacturer offers.

We do. Each of those cities has a Bighorn site of its own with detail specific to that market, and the crews are the same ones.
