Why local matters here

Lakewood is harder on paint than the metro, and most bids ignore that

Open prairie lots, almost no tree cover, wind off the plains and the full force of Colorado sun. A crew that mostly paints shaded Denver bungalows prices your house like a shaded Denver bungalow.

  • Wind drives the schedule. It dries coatings too fast and carries grit onto wet film, so elevations get sequenced by time of day instead of convenience.
  • Sun punishes the west side. On open lots the west and south elevations fade and chalk years before the rest of the house.
  • Irrigation soaks siding. Sprinklers and well systems on larger lots are the most common cause of early peeling we find out here.
  • Freeze-thaw opens joints. Caulk that was fine in October is a water entry point by April.
  • Fence runs are long. Lakewood subdivisions are full of privacy fence, and it weathers faster than anything else on the property.

The cost of doing it twice

A cheap repaint that skips washing, scraping and priming usually looks fine through the first season. By the second or third winter the trim is peeling and the sunny wall is chalking.

Then the next painter has to remove the failed coating before starting, which is work nobody paid for the first time. You end up paying for the cheap job, the removal, and the job done properly.

That is the entire reason our preparation is itemized in writing instead of buried in a lump sum.

Lakewood painting questions, answered straight

Who are the best painters in Lakewood, CO?

Compare documents rather than adjectives: a certificate of insurance, a written scope naming the preparation steps and the exact product and sheen per surface, the number of coats, and the warranty terms. Bighorn Painting provides all four, carries over 20 years of experience and backs every project with a 5-year workmanship warranty. Call 720-912-4676.

How much does it cost to paint a house in Lakewood?

Published 2026 Front Range cost data puts exterior house painting at roughly $1.55 to $4.10 per square foot of paintable surface, and interior painting at about $1.50 to $3.50 per square foot. Cabinets commonly run $2,000 to $8,000. Condition, height and trim detail move the number more than square footage alone.

What areas do you serve besides Lakewood?

Golden, Wheat Ridge, Morrison, Edgewater, Littleton, Ken Caryl, Evergreen and west Denver, plus every Lakewood neighborhood from Belmar to Solterra.

Are you insured?

Yes, fully insured, with proof of coverage provided at every quote. Colorado does not issue a statewide painting license, so insurance and a written scope are the two documents every homeowner should ask any painter to produce.

What does the 5-year warranty cover?

Our workmanship: the preparation and the application. If our work fails inside five years, we come back and correct it. The coating also carries the manufacturer's own warranty from Sherwin-Williams, PPG or Behr.

When can exteriors be painted in Lakewood?

Generally mid-May through early October. Denver-area climate records put the average last spring freeze near May 5 and the first fall freeze near October 7, and paint labels include overnight lows, not just daytime highs.

Do you paint commercial and agricultural buildings?

Yes. Offices, retail, restaurants, warehouses, shops, barns and metal outbuildings across Lakewood and the surrounding farm towns, with phased or after-hours scheduling.
