Contact questions

How do I reach Bighorn Painting?

Call 720-912-4676 any day of the week, send the quote form on this page, or email us. Quotes are free, written and carry no obligation.

How soon will I hear back?

Most calls and forms get a response the same day or the next. Written quotes usually follow within a day or two of scoping.

What should I include with my request?

Name and phone are all that is required. Adding the property address, what needs painting, the year built, your timeline and photos gets you a faster, more accurate number.

Do you take commercial inquiries here?

Yes. Commercial, agricultural and multi-unit projects start with a walkthrough so scope, access and phasing are understood before a number is quoted.

Which towns do you serve?

Lakewood and every neighborhood in it, plus Golden, Wheat Ridge, Morrison, Edgewater, Littleton, Ken Caryl, Evergreen and west Denver.
