In short

Bighorn Painting provides free written estimates throughout Lakewood, Colorado, in person or from photos. Every estimate names the surfaces, itemizes preparation by surface, categorizes and counts stucco crack repair, specifies the exact product line, sheen and permeability, states the coat count, includes any required staging, and gives the schedule, terms and written 5-year workmanship warranty. Call 720-912-4676.

Three ways to get a number

  1. Photos. Fastest. One shot of each elevation, close-ups of anything that worries you, and the year the home was built.
  2. Phone. Call 720-912-4676 and talk it through.
  3. In person. Necessary for stucco cracking, peeling paint, suspected water intrusion and commercial scopes.

What your estimate contains

  • A list of exactly which surfaces are in, and a plain sentence about anything deliberately left out.
  • Prep broken out surface by surface, so the word prep never has to carry the whole paragraph.
  • Crack repair categorized and counted on stucco work.
  • The exact Sherwin-Williams or PPG line and sheen per surface, with permeability noted on masonry coatings.
  • A number of coats, written as a number.
  • Any staging a hillside lot or vaulted interior requires.
  • When we begin, how many days we are on site, how payment works, and the five-year warranty attached.

What helps us price it accurately

Tell us the build year first, since anything before 1978 changes what the law requires of us. Then whether the exterior is stucco or siding, and if stucco, what the cracking looks like. Mention an association if there is one. Rough room counts or dimensions help. Above all, send pictures, which communicate more in ten seconds than a paragraph does.

What we will tell you that others might not

If your cracks look structural, you hear it before you spend anything on coating. If a wall is taking water, we will point at where. If your project honestly belongs in spring, we will say so, even though it means not booking work today.

Questions

About estimates

Is the estimate free?

Completely. Site visit or photo review, either way there is no charge and nobody chases you afterwards.

How fast do you respond?

Most requests get a reply before the end of the day, and never later than the next working day.

Can you quote without visiting?

Frequently yes, if the photos are decent and the job is a standard interior or a simple exterior. Anything with stucco movement, lifting paint or a commercial scope really does need eyes on it before we put a number on paper.

What should I photograph?

Stand back far enough to catch a whole wall in one frame, one shot per side of the house, then move in close on anything that looks wrong. Inside, one photo per room taken from the doorway does the job.

Will I get follow-up calls?

None. You get your answers and then the ball sits in your court. Comparing three quotes properly takes time and we would rather you did it.
