In short

Insured, rated 4.9 stars from 95 Google reviews, specifying Sherwin-Williams and PPG products, with a written 5-year workmanship warranty. Office at 12191 W 64th Ave Unit 102-8, Arvada, CO 80004.

How we work

  • The job is mostly what happens before the colour. Rolling paint is the easy part. Eight good years versus three is decided by the hours nobody photographs.
  • Specification before price. On a stucco house, choosing the coating correctly matters more than any discount. We will explain why we chose what we chose.
  • Bad news early. If your cracks look structural or your wall is taking water, you hear it at the estimate. That makes our number higher than a bidder who stays quiet.
  • If it matters, it is typed out. Which surfaces, which prep on each, which product, which sheen, how many coats, what days, what terms, what warranty.

What a project looks like

  1. Get in touch. Phone or photos, whichever suits. Say what is bothering you and when the house was built.
  2. We scope it. Photos are often enough. Stucco cracking, peeling and commercial work get a walkthrough.
  3. Written estimate. Everything named, including crack repair and any staging a hillside lot requires.
  4. Colors and approvals. Samples on your actual walls, and any HOA submission prepared before scheduling.
  5. Cover everything, then prep. This is the stage that determines how the whole thing ages.
  6. Coat it. Inside the manufacturer's conditions, elevations sequenced around sun and wind, cleaned up daily.
  7. Walk it with us. Anything you point at gets sorted before the final invoice. Then the warranty runs for five years.

Who we work for

Homeowners across every Lakewood neighborhood, HOA boards running phased community cycles, property managers with turnover work, and businesses around Belmar, Union Boulevard and the Colfax corridor.
